# Guided tips and editor dialogs

Guided feature tips wait while an editor modal dialog or the AI bar and its settings are open, keeping their actions clear. Closing the last open dialog lets a waiting guide resume when its associated control is still available. Opening a dialog does not mark the guide as completed or dismiss it.

Use the guide's usual Close or Got it action to dismiss it. Guides keep their normal order. Connection, session and license warnings keep their existing visibility.

A tip without a title omits its heading. A supplied title keeps its localized New label without accumulating another label when shown again.
